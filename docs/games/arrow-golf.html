Arrow Golf

Jay D. Mann · DREAMER no. 5 (1981-01) p. 15 1K RAM

Nine holes of golf in which an arrow replaces the club. Aim the arrow, hold 9 to store power and let go: the arrow flies a distance proportional to the beeps. Barriers bounce it back and cost an extra stroke, and the putt goes in when the arrow overlaps the square target.

Keys

Aim with 4/5/6/8/A/C/D/E around 9; hold 9 to build power and release to shoot.
