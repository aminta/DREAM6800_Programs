Indy 6800

S. Dixon · DREAMER no. 19 (1982) p. 5 1K RAM

Two car-race games in one: choose A for a straight road with oncoming cars or B for a winding road. Steer with 4 and 6, accelerate with E and brake with C (only down to the initial speed). After a crash the score shows, with HIGH SCORE if it is the best of the series.

Keys

A straight road or B winding road, 4/6 steer, E accelerate, C brake.
