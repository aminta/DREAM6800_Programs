Hangman (Ford)

Lindsay R. Ford · DREAMER no. 12 (1981-08) p. 9 2K RAM

A two-player hangman: the setter chooses a word length of 5 to 8 and keys in the letters, then the guesser tries letters. Each miss builds the hanged man and as many misses as letters hangs him; a correct guess wins a reprieve.

Keys

5-8 = word length, then each letter as two keys (A = 0A ... Z = 23).
