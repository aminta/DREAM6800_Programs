UFO Intercept

Michael J. Bauer · Electronics Australia (1979), cassette 1K RAM

Two UFOs, a big one and a small one, cross the sky; shoot them down with missiles from your base. The small UFO is worth more. The score is shown at the end of the game. Adapted from the RCA COSMAC VIP manual.

Keys

4, 5, 6 launch a missile to the left, straight up or to the right.
