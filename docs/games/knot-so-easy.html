Knot-So-Easy

R. Cameron · DREAMER no. 11 (1981-07) p. 19 1K RAM

A picture puzzle: a rope drawn as dots in a 4x4 block of squares is jumbled and rotated by the computer. You swap and rotate squares to rebuild it, then press 6 to check. The picture flashes off if wrong and a tone sounds if correct.

Keys

Co-ordinates x 1-4 then y 1-4 swap a square with its right neighbour, 5 rotates, 6 checks.
