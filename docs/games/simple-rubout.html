Simple Rubout

Bruce Mitchell · DREAMER no. 6 (1981-02) p. 10 1K RAM

Two players take turns erasing rectangles of dots, whose top left corner is the flashing cursor. Overlapping a gap earns a low beep and gives the opponent two turns. Whoever erases the last dot wins.

Keys

Two hex digits: width, then height of the rectangle.
