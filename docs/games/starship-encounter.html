Starship Encounter

Edward Perati · DREAMER no. 13 (1981-09) p. 13 1K RAM

Your fighter orbits a central starship while an enemy wanders randomly. Shoot the enemy with the horizontal laser, or fire the starship's force field to destroy anything near it. You start with 10 fighters and 20 hits earn a new game.

Keys

E starts and switches on the force field, 8/A/5/D move the fighter, F fires the laser.
