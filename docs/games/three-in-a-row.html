Three In A Row

K. Semrad · DREAMER no. 17 (1982-01) 1K RAM

A variation of Connect 4 for two players in which you need only three marbles of your colour in a line. Marbles are hollow or solid, and a full column gives a beep.

Keys

1-6 drop a marble in that column; C restarts after a win.
