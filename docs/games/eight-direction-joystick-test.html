Eight Direction Joystick Test

Graeme V. Samways · DREAMER no. 9 (1981-05) p. 13 1K RAM

A test program for the eight-direction paddle wired across keys 5/8/A/D. It shows the direction value (9 in the centre) at the top left, and an F when fire is held. The routine at 0230 is reused by the eight-direction version of Sub.

Keys

Paddle (or keys) 5 up, D down, 8 left, A right, two adjacent keys = diagonal, F = fire.
