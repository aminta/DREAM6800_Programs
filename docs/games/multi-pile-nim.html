Multi-Pile Nim

J. Fisher · DREAMER no. 14 (1981-10) p. 14 1K RAM

Nim against the computer with up to 15 piles of up to 15 objects, where you take any number from one pile per turn. The computer always moves first and the board is shown after its move, with white bands marking empty piles.

Keys

Number of piles (0-F), then each size; then pile and amount to take; F after the end returns to the monitor.
