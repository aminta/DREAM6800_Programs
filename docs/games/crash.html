Crash

Tony Horncastle · DREAMER no. 13 (1981-09) p. 17 1K RAM

Two cars race round a four-lane track in opposite directions. Car A scores for crashing into B, while B scores for each quarter lap. You drive one car while the DREAM drives the other, changing lanes at random, and the first to 250 points wins.

Keys

A or B picks the car the DREAM drives; 0 (you are A) or 3 (you are B) changes lane.
