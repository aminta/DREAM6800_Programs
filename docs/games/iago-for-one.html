Iago for One

M. Collins · DREAMER no. 10 (1981-06) p. 21 1K RAM

An add-on that lets one player compete against the DREAM at Reversi. The machine is slow, thinking for about 25 seconds, but it plays a tricky game. Its chosen move must be confirmed with F.

Keys

As Iago for Two: 0-7 for X then Y, F confirms, C changes turn.
