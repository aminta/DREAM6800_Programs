Spiral Trial

P.E. Marston · DREAMER no. 2 (1980-10) p. 13 1K RAM

The computer draws a spiral and a countdown timer starts as you appear in its centre. Steer out through the gaps without touching a wall: a crash sends you back to the start. The game ends at the exit (time left is your score), after 5 crashes or when the timer hits 000.

Keys

Hold 4 left, 6 right, 1 up, 9 down.
