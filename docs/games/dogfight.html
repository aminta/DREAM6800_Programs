Dogfight

G. Clare · DREAMER no. 17 (1982-01) 1K RAM

Fly a fighter and bring the enemy into your sights; each hit is worth 5 points and shots remaining are shown top right. A white line moving across the top is the time limit: the game ends when it reaches the right or the shots run out.

Keys

8 bank left, A bank right, D dive, 5 climb, F fire, 0 restart.
