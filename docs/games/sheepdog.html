Sheepdog

K. Semrad · DREAMER no. 15 (1981-11) p. 8 1K RAMjoystickplayable, see notes

Use the joystick to drive a dog and herd sheep into the paddock at the top left. A bark makes the nearest sheep run away from the dog. One sheep is lame, the others bolt away and may vanish into the distance as a dot.

Keys

Bauer's joystick moves the dog, F barks.
