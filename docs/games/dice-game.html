Dice Game

K. Semrad · DREAMER no. 10 (1981-06) p. 21 1K RAM

Place a bet of 1 to 10 units and call whether two dice will total over or under seven. The bank wins whenever a seven is thrown. The game ends when you go broke or break the bank.

Keys

Bet 1-9 or A (=10); A = over 7, B = under 7; 0 clears the table; C after the end.
