Three Dimensional Noughts and Crosses

D. Woolnough · DREAMER no. 15 (1981-11) p. 17 1K RAM

Three noughts-and-crosses boards side by side, with a win needing one symbol on each board in symmetrical positions. The program only draws the marks and alternates the players, so the players must judge the win themselves. The three boards can also be played individually.

Keys

Row A/B/C then column 1-9 (1-3 left board, 4-6 middle, 7-9 right).
