Duck Shoot

C. Fethers · DREAMER no. 11 (1981-07) p. 21 1K RAM

A duck bounces around the screen while you move a gun sight and fire; you have 20 rounds. After each shot the rounds left are shown, and a hit duck dies. At the end the total bag is displayed.

Keys

8 left, A right, 5 up, 1 down, F fire.
