Wipe Off

Michael J. Bauer · Electronics Australia (1979), cassette 1K RAM

Knock all the dots off the screen with the ball and bat, a CHIP-8 take on Breakout. The score is shown after 20 balls. Bauer's tip: a smaller bat with 02CD = F0, a bat with a hole with E7. Adapted from the RCA COSMAC VIP manual.

Keys

4 and 6 serve the ball and move the bat.
