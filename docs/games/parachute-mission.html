Parachute Mission

F. Lever (Jnr) · DREAMER no. 6 (1981-02) p. 11 1K RAM

Jump from the platform and float to the ground while dodging a randomly moving mine and two patches of quicksand. Losing needs a key to restart; winning restarts by itself.

Keys

F jump, C steer left, D steer right.
