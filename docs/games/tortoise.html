Tortoise

P.E. Marston · DREAMER no. 8 (1981-04) p. 19 1K RAM

Drive a car round a track: five laps or five crashes end the game. A crash stops the car until you back out. At the end a skill message is shown and the game restarts on a key.

Keys

Any key starts; 8 left, A right, 5 up, D down.
