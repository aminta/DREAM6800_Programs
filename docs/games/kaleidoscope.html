Kaleidoscope

Joseph Weisbecker, adapted by Michael J. Bauer · Electronics Australia (1979), cassette 1K RAM

Type a short sequence of moves and the DREAM keeps repeating it, mirrored four ways, to build a moving symmetrical pattern. Adapted from the RCA COSMAC VIP manual.

Keys

2, 4, 6, 8 enter a short sequence of moves, 0 starts it. Bauer suggests 44444442220.
