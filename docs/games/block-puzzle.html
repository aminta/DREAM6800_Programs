Block Puzzle

Michael J. Bauer · Electronics Australia (1979), cassette 1K RAM

The sixteen hex digits sit in order on a 4×4 board with one empty square. The DREAM shuffles them, then you slide the blocks back into order, one move at a time.

Keys

2 down, 4 left, 6 right, 8 up: slide a block into the empty square.
