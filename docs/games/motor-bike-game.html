Motor Bike Game

C. Fethers · DREAMER no. 10 (1981-06) p. 23 1K RAMplayable, see notes

Ride a motorbike with the speed, distance and time shown at the top. Change gears by lifting off the accelerator, and avoid pulling a wheelie by accelerating too hard. Jump or brake for obstacles; the trip ends after 120 miles.

Keys

D accelerate, E brake, F foot.
