Maze

A. Groves · DREAMER no. 17 (1982-01) 1K RAM

Guide a mouse from the top right of a maze to the cheese at the bottom left. Hitting a wall sends it back to the start and costs one of five turns, and there is also a random time limit. If you run out of turns or time, the cat eats the mouse and the program halts.

Keys

Hold 1 up, 9 down, 4 left, 6 right.
