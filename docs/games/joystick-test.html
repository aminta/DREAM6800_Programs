Joystick Test

M.J. Bauer · DREAMER no. 3 (1980-11) p. 17 1K RAMjoystick

Bauer's service routine for the home-made analog joystick, followed by a small test program. A dot follows the stick on the screen while its X and Y values are printed in decimal. Other joystick games reuse the same routine.

Keys

Needs Bauer's analog joystick; no keys.
